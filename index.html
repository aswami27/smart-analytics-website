About Us

We started Smart Analytics because most Indian businesses we met had plenty of data and very little use for it.

Smart Analytics is a data analytics consultancy based in India. We work with organisations that generate a lot of operational data — sales figures, transaction logs, store or distribution numbers — but don't yet have the internal team to turn that into something usable. Our work has included Dabur in FMCG, Vishal Mega Mart in retail, and a number of MSMEs that are analytics-curious but resource-constrained.

Across finance, retail, sales, FMCG and beyond, the underlying problem tends to repeat: the data exists, but it lives in disconnected spreadsheets, legacy reports or systems nobody fully trusts. Our job is to make that data legible — clean it, structure it, and build the reporting or analysis layer that lets people actually make decisions with it.

We keep engagements scoped to a real business question rather than selling a fixed "analytics package." Some clients need a single dashboard rebuilt properly; others need ongoing support as an outsourced analytics function. Both are common.
